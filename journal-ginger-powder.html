Ginger Powder: From Product Brief to Application

Questions to ask about format, treatment and suitability for food and beverage production.

Explain the formulation need

Ginger powder may be considered for spice blends, bakery recipes and other food or beverage applications. Share the intended use and desired sensory profile so that a suitable product can be discussed.

02

Ask for the technical details

Review product identity, ingredient composition, particle size, moisture, treatment and storage information. Discuss any microbiological or contaminant testing requirements relevant to your product brief with the supplier before placing the order.

03

Evaluate before scaling

Use a representative sample in your own formulation and processing conditions. Confirm that the agreed commercial specification reflects the product evaluated, then settle packaging, quantity and supporting documentation requirements.
