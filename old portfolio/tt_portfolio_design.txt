OUR STORY: FROM SOIL TO SOUL

Tropic Treasure Trade bridges Kerala’s ancient spice heritage with modern wellness lifestyles. Rooted in the sacred Western Ghats and managed from our Brighton base, we deliver premium spices, nuts, and wellness products with full traceability and ethical integrity.

We work directly with organic cooperatives and small-holder farmers, ensuring every product is:
	•	Authentic & Pure: Single-origin, unadulterated botanical excellence
	•	Fairly Traded: Directly supporting those who tend the soil
	•	Premium Quality: Hand-selected for color, size, and essential oil content


The Three Pillars
	1.	Maximum Potency: Frequent, small-batch imports to preserve flavor and essential oils
	2.	The Ethical Pact: Sourcing exclusively from verified organic cooperatives
	3.	Radical Transparency: 100% natural, pesticide-free, and fully traceable products
