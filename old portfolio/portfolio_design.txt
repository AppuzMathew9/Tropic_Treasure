🌱 OUR STORY: FROM SOIL TO SOUL
Tropic Treasure was born from a simple truth: the world’s greatest luxuries are sun, soil, and the hands that tend them.

Rooted in the sacred landscapes of the Western Ghats, Kerala, and managed from our coastal base in Brighton, we bridge the gap between ancient spice heritage and the modern British wellness seeker. We don’t just move cargo; we move culture. By working directly with 18,000+ organic small-holder farmers, we ensure that every gram of spice tells a story of ethical empowerment and uncompromising purity.

Our Three Pillars:

Maximum Potency: We import in frequent, small batches to ensure essential oils are at their peak when they reach your kitchen.
The 18,000+ Pact: We source exclusively from organic cooperatives, ensuring fair trade and long-term soil health.
Radical Transparency: 100% natural, pesticide-free, and traditionally processed. No fillers, no shortcuts.

page visual layout strategy for your Tropic Treasure catalogue:

Page 1: The "Hero" Entrance (The Origin)

Background: A stunning, full-page, "faded" high-resolution image of a Kerala backwater at sunrise (misty, calm, green).

Overlay Text: Place your logo in the centre-top. Underneath, in a clean gold or white font: "The Bridge to the Tropics: From the Western Ghats to the British Coast."

Visual Trick: Keep the bottom 20% of the page solid white or deep forest green to house your "Soil to Soul" intro text.


Page 2: The Sourcing Story (The People)

Left Side (60%): A "lifestyle" shot of a Wayanad farmer tending to black pepper vines or drying ginger. This builds immediate trust.

Right Side (40%): Your "Story" and "Three Pillars" text.

Small Inset Image: A small, polaroid-style photo of your mother (the Director) at the Kerala homestead to add the "Family-Owned" touch.


Page 3: The Signature Collection (The Treasure)

Grid Layout: Divide this page into a clean 2x3 grid.

The Content:
Square 1: A close-up, high-detail macro shot of Green Cardamom pods on a dark stone background.

Square 2: The product description for Cardamom (clean text, no border).

Square 3: The description for Tellicherry Pepper.

Square 4: A macro shot of the Extra Bold Peppercorns.

Visual Connection: Run a thin, "water-style" wavy line across the middle of the page to represent the backwaters, subtly connecting the spices.

Page 4: Trust & Contact (The Professionalism)

Top Half: A panoramic, blurred image of a traditional Kerala houseboat (Kettuvallam).

Middle Section: The "Trust & Verification Panel." Use clean icons (Shield for Lab tests, Map for Traceability, Barcode for Retail-ready).

Bottom Section: Solid white background with your Brighton address and the QR Code.

Final Visual: A small UK flag and Indian flag side-by-side at the very bottom with the text: "Kerala Heritage | British Excellence."

Design Pro-Tips for TTT:

The "White Space" Rule: Don't fill every corner. Let the images "breathe." In the UK, white space equals Luxury.

Consistency: Use the same "Green" from your Kerala backwater photos as the accent colour for your text headings.

Paper Quality: If you print this for UK trade fairs, use Matte Recycled Paper. It feels premium and matches your "Sustainable" brand identity.
