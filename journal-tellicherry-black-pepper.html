Understanding Tellicherry Black Pepper TGSEB

A buyer’s introduction to grade, treatment and the specification behind a pepper enquiry.

The essentials

At a glance

  • ✓Specify origin, grade and treatment together.
  • ✓Check the current specification and supporting evidence.
  • ✓Connect sample approval to the commercial batch.
01

Define the product clearly

For a TGSEB black peppercorn enquiry, identify the required origin, whole-pepper format and grade. Tropic Treasure’s initial sourcing focus is Kerala-origin TGSEB black peppercorns. Buyers should ask for the current technical specification rather than relying on a product photograph.

Buyer’s perspective

Specify origin, grade and treatment together.

↗
02

Review quality and treatment

Discuss size requirements, moisture, foreign matter and the treatment required for the intended application. Where steam sterilisation is requested, confirm that it applies to the product offered and obtain the relevant supporting information. Any organic requirement should be supported by current evidence applicable to the supply.

03

Connect the sample to the order

Evaluate an identified sample against your requirements, then agree how the commercial batch will be checked. Confirm the specification, testing requirements, packaging and documentation before order placement. Retain the lot references so that sample, test records and shipment can be connected.
