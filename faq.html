Frequently
Asked Questions

Useful information to help you with product enquiries,
orders, quality and international trade.

01

Can I request a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes, you can request a sample for evaluation. Sample availability depends on the product and grade. Please share your requirements so we can confirm sample options, quantities, any applicable charges and delivery arrangements.

02

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Minimum order quantities vary by product, grade and packaging format. We review each enquiry against available supply options and confirm the MOQ during the quotation process.

03

Can you arrange customised packaging or private labelling?

Yes, we can explore suitable packaging and private-label options, subject to supplier capabilities, order quantities and destination requirements.

04

What payment terms are available for international orders?

Payment arrangements are discussed during quotation and depend on the product, order value and agreed commercial terms.

05

What technical documents are available for international buyers?

Depending on the product and shipment, available documentation may include technical specifications, certificates of analysis, relevant testing reports and origin-related records. Specific requirements can be reviewed during the enquiry process.

06

How long does an international order take to fulfil?

Lead times depend on product availability, quantity, packaging, documentation and destination. An estimated timeline can be provided after reviewing your enquiry.
